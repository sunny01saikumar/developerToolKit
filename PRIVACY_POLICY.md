# Privacy Policy for DevToolkit Pro

**Effective Date: August 1, 2026**

DevToolkit Pro ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy describes how we handle information in our mobile application, **DevToolkit Pro** (the "App").

## 1. 100% Offline Operations & Data Security
DevToolkit Pro is a completely offline developer utility suite. 
- **No Data Collection:** The App does not collect, monitor, track, store, or transmit any data entered by you (such as JSON text, SQL queries, JWT tokens, generated passwords, notes, or search queries).
- **On-Device Storage:** All user configuration settings, favorites/bookmarks, notes, and tool history are stored locally on your physical device using secure Android Preferences DataStore. This data never leaves your device and is permanently deleted if you uninstall the App.

## 2. Permissions Used by the App
- **Camera Permission:** Used solely on-device to capture frames for the QR/Barcode Scanner utility using Android CameraX and local Google ML Kit analysis. Images are processed in real-time in the device memory and are never saved, stored, or transmitted over the network.
- **Internet Permission:** Used exclusively by the Google AdMob SDK to request and load advertisements.

## 3. Advertising & Third-Party Services
The App displays advertisements served by **Google AdMob**. AdMob may collect and use device identifiers (such as the Android Advertising ID) and approximate location data to serve personalized or non-personalized ads. 
- You can review Google's Privacy Policy here: [https://policies.google.com/privacy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)

## 4. Children's Privacy
Our App is intended for developers and is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone, including children.

## 5. Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy within the App or updating this file in our repository.

## 6. Contact Us
If you have any questions or feedback regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact us at: support@devtoolkit.com
